# About hibi.is

## What is hibi.is?

hibi.is is a modern, fast, and user-friendly platform for creating, storing, and sharing content. Whether you're a developer sharing code snippets, a writer publishing articles, a team collaborating on documents, or anyone who needs to quickly share information, hibi.is makes it simple and efficient.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to provide the fastest and most reliable way to share content online. We believe that sharing information should be:

- **Simple:** No complicated setup or configuration
- **Fast:** Create and share in seconds
- **Reliable:** Your content is always available when you need it
- **Secure:** Your data is protected with industry-standard security
- **Accessible:** Works on any device, anywhere

## Key Features

### 🚀 Speed & Simplicity

Created with performance in mind, hibi.is loads fast and responds instantly. No bloat, no unnecessary features—just what you need to get things done.

### 🔒 Privacy-First

We respect your privacy. Choose who can see your content with granular privacy controls. Your data belongs to you.

### 🛠️ Developer-Friendly

Built by developers, for developers. Full REST API, webhooks, SDKs in multiple languages, and extensive documentation.

### 🌍 Global Reach

Powered by a global CDN, your content is delivered quickly no matter where your audience is located.

### 📊 Analytics & Insights

Understand how your content is being used with detailed analytics (optional and privacy-respecting).

## Use Cases

hibi.is is versatile and can be used for:

- **Code Sharing:** Share code snippets with syntax highlighting
- **Link Shortening:** Create short, memorable URLs
- **Documentation:** Write and publish technical documentation
- **Note Taking:** Quick notes that you can access anywhere
- **Team Collaboration:** Share information with your team
- **Content Publishing:** Publish articles, guides, and tutorials
- **File Sharing:** Share files securely (Pro+)
- **API Integration:** Integrate with your applications and workflows

## Technology

hibi.is is built with modern, proven technologies:

- **Infrastructure:** Cloud-native, highly available architecture
- **Security:** TLS 1.3, encryption at rest, regular security audits
- **Performance:** Global CDN, edge caching, optimized delivery
- **Reliability:** 99.9% uptime SLA (Enterprise), automated backups
- **Scalability:** Auto-scaling infrastructure handles any load

## Open Source

We believe in open source. While our web application is proprietary, we've open-sourced:

- Client SDKs (JavaScript, Python, Go, Ruby, PHP)
- CLI tools
- Example applications
- Integration templates
- Documentation

Find our repositories at [github.com/guillaumemorin/hibi](https://github.com/guillaumemorin/hibi)

## Team

hibi.is is developed and maintained by a passionate team dedicated to building the best content sharing platform. We're distributed across the globe, working to serve users in every timezone.

## Privacy Policy

We take privacy seriously:

- **No tracking:** We don't track you across the web
- **No ads:** We're funded by our users, not advertisers
- **No data selling:** Your data is never sold to third parties
- **Transparency:** Clear privacy policy and terms of service
- **GDPR compliant:** Full compliance with data protection regulations
- **Data portability:** Export your data anytime

Read our full [Privacy Policy](https://hibi.is/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://hibi.is/terms).
